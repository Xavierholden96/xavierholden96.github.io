Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clip_Desk handles information when you use the Clip_Desk desktop content-management tool and related integrations.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, Clip_Desk may process account identifiers, public stream or clip metadata, chat or engagement signals, user-selected media files, generated transcripts, analysis results, approval decisions, and authentication tokens required to connect supported third-party services.

2. Local processing and storage

Clip_Desk is designed primarily as a desktop tool. Project data, candidate records, settings, and temporary media-processing files may be stored on the user's own device. Temporary media files may be removed after processing, depending on the feature used.

3. Third-party services

Clip_Desk may communicate with third-party services such as Twitch, TikTok, OpenAI, and GitHub when the user enables related features. Information sent to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms. Clip_Desk only requests permissions needed for the feature the user chooses to use.

4. Authentication credentials

OAuth access tokens and API credentials should be treated as confidential. Users should not publish or share them. Where supported, Clip_Desk uses authorization flows provided by the relevant platform rather than collecting platform passwords.

5. Media and content rights

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the rights, licenses, or permissions required to process, edit, upload, or publish media through Clip_Desk. Clip_Desk does not claim ownership of user content and does not grant users rights to third-party content.

6. Data sharing

Clip_Desk does not sell personal information. Data may be transmitted to enabled third-party APIs solely to provide requested functions such as authentication, analysis, transcription, or content uploading.

7. Retention and deletion

Because Clip_Desk primarily runs on the user's device, users can control local project data by removing the application's local files or database. Data already transmitted to third-party services may be subject to those services' retention rules.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to limit unnecessary exposure of credentials and user data. No software or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Clip_Desk is not intended for children under 13 and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as Clip_Desk evolves. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the Clip_Desk developer through GitHub at @Xavierholden96.
