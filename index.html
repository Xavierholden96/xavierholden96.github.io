Creator workflow software

Find the moments worth sharing.

Clip_Desk is a desktop content-management tool for creators and content teams. It helps monitor live-stream activity, surface promising moments, rank clips with engagement and AI signals, and prepare approved content for short-form publishing workflows.

Signal detection

Identify unusual audience activity and candidate moments without manually watching every second of a live stream.

Human review

Review, approve, or reject candidates before they move into a publishing workflow.

Content preparation

Prepare authorized clips for supported social platforms, including draft-based publishing flows.

How Clip_Desk works

Clip_Desk combines platform APIs, local media processing, and optional AI analysis to help a user move from live-stream moments to reviewed short-form content.

Approved, authorized media can be formatted for supported publishing workflows.

Built around user control

Clip_Desk is designed so publishing decisions remain with the user. Users are responsible for ensuring they own or have permission to use the media they process or publish. Clip_Desk does not grant rights to third-party content.
